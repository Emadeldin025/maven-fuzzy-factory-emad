
# 📈 Phase 1: Traffic Analysis & Optimization

## Overview
This phase focuses on analyzing website traffic sources and optimizing marketing performance based on conversion trends and user device types.

---

## 📷 Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard Preview](Traffic%20Sources.png)


## Key Analyses

### 🔹 Top Traffic Sources
- Identified most common `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, and `http_referer`.
- Ranked by total number of sessions.

### 🔹 Conversion Rate by Traffic Source
- Analyzed how different campaigns convert into orders.
- Focused on `gsearch` and `nonbrand`.

### 🔹 Weekly Traffic Trend
- Sessions grouped by week to monitor growth and traffic consistency.

### 🔹 Device Type Performance
- Conversion rates compared between `desktop` and `mobile`.

### 🔹 Device Segment Weekly Trend
- Mobile vs. Desktop traffic segmented and visualized by week.

## Dashboard Visuals
- Line chart: Sessions by month and source
- Bar chart: Campaign performance by device
- Cards: Sessions, Orders, CVR

---

## Business Insights
- Desktop traffic consistently showed higher conversion rates.
- Non-brand Google search traffic drives high quality visitors.
- Mobile traffic is increasing and may need UX optimization.
